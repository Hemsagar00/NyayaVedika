# Brand Blueprint: NyayaVedika (न्यायवेदिका)

> "Satyameva Jayate — Truth Alone Triumphs."

---

## 1. Identity & Etymology
- **Name**: **NyayaVedika**
- **Etymology**:
  - *Nyaya* (न्याय): Justice, Equity, Law, Logical Proof.
  - *Vedika* (वेदिका): An elevated platform, forum, dais, or council.
  - *Meaning*: **The Forum of Justice** — a prestigious, dignified, and democratic platform for legal intellect and courtroom mastery.
- **Domain**: `nyayavedika.in`
- **Tagline**: *India's Open Legal Intelligence, Case Repository & Advocate Workspace*

---

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Dignified & Scholarly**: Speaks with the authority of the High Bench and constitutional jurisprudence.
- **Clear & Uncompromising**: Avoids jargon traps; explains legal ratios with pristine lucidity.
- **Empowering**: Serves the junior advocate in mofussil courts with the same computational firepower as senior Supreme Court counsels.

---

## 3. Visual Identity & Color System
Replacing Supreme Today's aggressive maroon with a stately constitutional palette:

| Token | Hex | Role | Contrast vs Light BG |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-primary` | `#0f172a` | Midnight Navy — Primary text, judicial headers, core branding | 15.8:1 (AAA) |
| `--color-accent` | `#c29d59` | Sovereign Gold — Badges, citations, premium markers, highlights | 3.5:1 (UI accents) |
| `--color-bg` | `#faf9f5` | Warm Parchment — Reduces glare for multi-hour judgment reading | — |
| `--color-surface` | `#ffffff` | Pure Paper — Cards, judgment blocks, modals | — |
| `--color-muted` | `#64748b` | Slate Gray — Subheadings, metadata, citations | 4.8:1 (AA) |
| `--color-success` | `#059669` | Emerald — Disposed/allowed cases, confirmation badges | 4.6:1 (AA) |

---

## 4. Typography
- **Headings**: `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`, or `Gloock` (Serif) — conveying constitutional gravity and parliamentary tradition.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: `Inter` (Sans-Serif) — optimized for high readability, paragraph numbers, and dense statutes.
- **Legal Citations & Codes**: `JetBrains Mono` or tabular numerals for section numbers and SCC citations.

---

## 5. Trademark & Sweep Rules
The project codebase must contain no references to:
- `Supreme Today`, `SupremeToday`, `Supreme Today AI`
- `Court Diary`, `CourtDiary`, `courtdiary.app`
- Any third-party trademarked analytics tracking code.
All user-facing copy, logos, meta tags, and open-graph images belong strictly to **NyayaVedika**.
